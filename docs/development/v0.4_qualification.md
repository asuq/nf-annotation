# v0.4 qualification record

**Status, 2026-09-12:** The ultra-sensitive reference cohort passes all 25 native
tool results and independent checks of all five genomes and six matrices.
The requested switch to `sensitive` with iteration is implemented; its native
comparison is running, with ANI and portable-reannotation checks pending. This record
does not establish 10,000-sample performance or population-level annotation accuracy.

The approved scope and remaining gates are in the
[development specification](v0.4.md). The frozen ultra-sensitive reference
candidate is `a0c54b3`, including the bundle, publication and large-table fixes below;
native evidence generated with earlier candidates is retained and reinterpreted
only where the recorded search identity remains compatible.
Execution paths, scheduler identifiers, complete logs and acquisition records
are retained in ignored local qualification records.

The user selected the `sensitive` ceiling with iteration after identifying a
10,000-sample production target. Resource bytes, runtimes, E-value and downstream
confidence policies remain unchanged. The prospective smoke set contains, per
genome, five lowest-identity native seed matches, three distinct highest-score
matches and two previously unmapped proteins. Its enriched composition tests the
changed command and difficult cases; its proportions are not a cohort-wide
sensitivity estimate. The pinned mapper documents `sensitive` with iteration
as its default in the [v3 usage guide](https://github.com/eggnogdb/eggnog-mapper/blob/v3.0.0-beta6/USAGE.md).

The current implementation runs native searches per proteome and retains all
cohort protein and detailed annotation rows in aggregation memory. Batched-search
equivalence, aggregation memory and output/storage growth require separate
measurement before claiming suitability for the requested 10,000 samples.

The translation audit extracts each CDS from the native Prokka GenBank DNA in
biological order, applies its `codon_start` and selected translation table,
validates alternative initiation codons, and compares the complete amino-acid
sequence with the original FAA. This checks native gene-model consistency;
source ambiguity masking is audited separately.
For example, G37's Prokka-labelled DnaJ contains a TGA codon at source-contig
`L43967.2:2071-2073` (1-based closed), retained as residue 82, tryptophan.

## Native interpretation checks

The eggNOG positive input contains three public M129 proteins
(`WP_010874359.1`, `WP_010874360.1`, `WP_010874363.1`) plus a poly-proline
negative control. The native search maps and accepts the three biological
proteins and leaves the negative control unmapped. An independent single-protein
negative run returns zero hits with valid native headers and GO sidecar.

Native EC values carry an `ec:` namespace. The parser validates that namespace,
retains the raw field and exports canonical EC numbers. The positive control
retains `5.6.2.2` and `2.7.4.9`. Native `COG_category` fields instead contain
`COG0484`, `COG0187` and `COG0125`; all three fields are quarantined. Their valid
KO, EC and other fields remain usable. Category letters are not inferred from
those IDs or borrowed from COGclassifier.

All returned GO namespace confidence values in this native positive control are
low or unannotated, so none enters primary GO counts. Separate synthetic
controls test high/medium/low confidence independently for each GO namespace.
Raw outputs retain their original checksums after parser replay.

G37 adds real high/medium/low GO evidence in every namespace. An independent
per-gene comparison of its native namespace sidecar with normalized evidence
passes: 68 proteins retain 136 gene-GO assignments covering 42 terms. Fifteen
proteins have high or medium confidence in one namespace and low confidence in
another. For example, gene `GCA0000273251_00038` has high molecular-function and
biological-process confidence but low cellular-component confidence; the latter
terms do not enter primary counts. The 507 native namespace rows represent
annotated queries; the search found 511 seeds among 522 input proteins.

### Source-specific KO disagreements

Among genes with accepted KOs from both callers, G37 has one disjoint assignment
set out of 253, M129 has two out of 278, Spiroplasma has two out of 126, and
Holdemania has one out of 229. OY-M's eggNOG result remains pending.
These differences were checked against the native rows on identical proteins.
For example, the G37/M129 glycerol-3-phosphate-oxidase-labelled proteins retain
eggNOG K00111, while KOfam accepts K28477 and leaves K00111 below its threshold.
The M129 and Spiroplasma glutamyl-tRNA-ligase-labelled proteins retain eggNOG
K09698, while KOfam accepts K01885 and leaves K09698 below its threshold. The
pipeline preserves each caller's evidence without choosing a consensus KO.

Spiroplasma protein `GCA9652265251_00319` illustrates why disjoint labels need
inspection. Its 386-residue native gene model contains PF03602 at residues
1-181 and a guanylate-kinase domain, PF00625, at 196-371. The retained RPS-BLAST
evidence maps COG0742/RsmD to 1-183 and the primary COG0194/Gmk hit to 197-366.
EggNOG accepts K00942; KOfam accepts K08316 while its K00942 score remains below
threshold. The different domain regions help explain the distinct calls. This
is a predicted two-domain gene model, not experimental evidence of both
activities or proof against assembly/gene-prediction error. The secondary COG
hit and below-threshold KO are not promoted into primary assignments.

Holdemania's `GCA0001579951_02223` retains eggNOG K03483 at medium confidence.
KOfam's K03483 domain score is below threshold (367.9 versus 456.90), while
K02821 passes (134.3 versus 102.70). Its native Pfam evidence includes Mga,
PRD and PTS_EIIA_2 domains, and COGclassifier assigns COG3711. These support
inspection at the domain level without establishing a particular transported
substrate. The native preferred name `ulaC` has low confidence and remains
excluded from primary preferred-name evidence.

The strict GO confidence filter retains only two Holdemania genes, with four
gene-GO assignments covering three terms. Independent comparison with all
3,366 native namespace rows passes. There are no high-confidence namespaces;
medium confidence occurs once each in molecular function and biological process
and twice in cellular component. The remaining annotated namespaces are low
confidence, so sparse primary GO counts must not be interpreted as biological
absence of those functions. EggNOG maps 3,433 of 3,470 proteins and accepts
functional evidence for 2,842; primary KO and EC counts cover 335 and 134 genes,
respectively. Its 2,381 field errors concern only the malformed native COG
category field.

## Resource measurements and outstanding checks

Both completed eggNOG controls used 16 CPUs, 64 GB requested memory, the full
resource, `--ultra-sensitive --iterate --algo 1`, top-hit mode 0, E-value 0.001,
block size 7.0 and four index chunks. Resource directories were mounted
read-only. GNU `time` measured the complete native command:

| Control | Native wall time | User + system CPU seconds | Maximum RSS, KiB | Native exit |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Four proteins, three positives | 01:46:54 | 73,325.00 | 15,804,608 | 0 |
| One negative protein | 01:26:10 | 66,229.66 | 15,794,872 | 0 |

The original four-CPU eggNOG control exceeded a one-hour allocation. These
measurements do not qualify the public four-CPU/32-GB default or establish a
minimum memory requirement. Cold and warm cache effects were not isolated.
Small input size does not make this full-resource search cheap.

The successful G37 eggNOG Slurm task took 01:52:46 elapsed and 24:45:31 total
CPU time, with a recorded maximum RSS of 15,760,997 KiB. The native mapper
reports 6,726 seconds. In the recovered workflow, KOfamScan took 00:01:47
elapsed and PADLOC 00:02:33; the eggNOG, COGclassifier and Pfam native outputs
were reused and renormalized. The independent cohort checker passed all six
matrices. The original failed workflow and its two native failures remain
preserved separately from the complete recovered cohort.

The five-genome profile keeps 16 CPUs and 64 GB per functional search,
allows four concurrent searches and allocates four hours on the compute queue.
Its explicit process queue was checked against actual scheduler submission;
the short partition has a two-hour association limit. Upstream processes retain
their separate limits. The initial five-genome run resolved the site submission
limit to 2,000 despite `params.executor_queue_size = 16`; the recovery overlay
sets `executor.queueSize = 16` explicitly. Functional search concurrency remains
bounded separately by `annotation_max_forks = 4`. Work-directory allocation
and apparent size are sampled every 30 seconds, excluding linked databases.
The maximum observed footprint is a lower bound on peak storage; files deleted
between samples and deleted-but-open files are not measured by these directory
snapshots. Four initial-run samples retain an explicit unavailable measurement
because CRISPRCasFinder or GNU parallel removed temporary files during `du`;
these are measurement races, not workflow failures or zero-byte observations.
A separate complete, single-time inspection of the active OY-M job's batch
processes found 26 deleted-but-open files totalling 8,192 allocated bytes.
That snapshot does not establish a peak or repair gaps in the directory series.

Before closing qualification, record the completed five-genome counts,
gene/coordinate and CRISPR reconciliation, ANI behaviour under both existing
settings, portable published-result reuse, and elapsed/CPU/RSS/storage/output
measurements. Five genomes do not establish performance for a substantially
larger production cohort. Optional MGE markers are omitted from this release.
